Start early. Prepare well.

One lesson I want to keep practicing is doing important tasks as early as I can. Preparation gives me time to understand what is expected, spot what I still need to learn, and handle surprises without rushing at the last minute.

I want to bring that habit into my studies and future engineering projects. Planning ahead gives me more room to think, ask questions, and do the work with care.

Personal reflection·Study habits

Preparation is part of the work

Starting early helps me see the steps a task will take instead of waiting until the deadline is close. I can break the work into smaller parts, look for help when I need it, and check my understanding before I submit anything.

Looking ahead·AI Engineering

Building habits I can bring into engineering

Engineering projects need planning as well as technical skills. I want to practice preparing in advance, learning from mistakes, and leaving time to review my work as I move through college.
